I am a second-year PhD student in the [Clark Group](https://clark.physics.illinois.edu) at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, working with [Bryan Clark](https://physics.illinois.edu/people/faculty/bryan-clark). My research sits at the intersection of **quantum many-body physics**, **quantum information**, and **computational methods**.

I work on problems involving:
- Fixed-Node Diffusion Monte Carlo (FN-DMC) and its theoretical foundations
- Measurement-induced phase transitions (MIPTs) and entanglement dynamics
- Quantum simulation and resource estimation for strongly correlated systems

Prior to UIUC, I completed my MSc in Physics at the [University of British Columbia](https://www.ubc.ca) and the [Stewart Blusson Quantum Matter Institute](https://qmi.ubc.ca), where I was supervised by [Roman Krems](https://groups.chem.ubc.ca/krems/).

Outside of research, I enjoy cycling, playing cricket, hiking, and spending time with friends and family.
